# HRCC HF Net

This is a casual directed net, run by volunteers of the HRCC community. This net meets on Fridays at 9:30PM Eastern, 6:30PM Pacific, 0130 UTC. The net usually meets on the 40m band, operating Single Sideband (SSB).

Since conditions are not always ideal, there will often be stations attempting to relay the far reaches of North America back to Net Control.

Often times, after the official net, participants may move to smaller coordinated contact groups using other bands or other modes.
