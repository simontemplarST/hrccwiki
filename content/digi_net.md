# HRCC Digital Modes Net

This is a weekly digital mode net, run by volunteers from the HRCC community. The net utilizes the HRCC Reflector, an actual piece of hardware that connects the supported digital modes together. See the [HRCC Link](https://hrcc.link/) site for details.

The net meets weekly on Thursdays at 9:30PM Eastern, 6:30PM Pacific, 0130 UTC.

## Supported Modes

### Digital Mobile Radio(DMR)

### D-Star

### Yeasu System Fusion (YSF)

### Wires-X

### M17
