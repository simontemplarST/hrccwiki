# GMRS

The **General Mobile Radio Service** (**GMRS**) is a land-mobile FM UHF service designed for short-distance two-way communication and authorized under FCC Part 95 in the United States.

GMRS licenses can be obtained from the FCC Universal Licensing system for the cost of $35. Licenses are valid for 10 years, and can be used by the licensee as well as their immediate family. Proper identification is required in 15 minute intervals and at the end of the conversation.
