## Contributing to the Wiki

This is a **community wiki** — anyone can suggest an edit or add a page, and changes are published automatically.

1.  Click **Edit this page** at the bottom of any page (or [browse the source on GitHub](https://github.com/simontemplarST/hrccwiki/tree/main/content)).
2.  Make your change in the editor. GitHub will fork the repo and open a **pull request** for you.
3.  Once opened, edits and additions to wiki content are **accepted and published automatically**, usually within a minute.

To start a brand-new page, add a `.md` file under the `content/` folder in the appropriate section.

Any questions or concerns? Reach out to **@Staff** in the [HRCC Discord](https://discord.com/invite/YGTJx8E).
