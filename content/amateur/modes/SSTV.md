# SSTV

## Slow-scan Television

Slow-scan Television is a modulation mode that allows for the transfer of images on amateur radio frequencies.  SSTV is classified as a phone transmission, and as such is transmitted on frequencies located in the phone portions (LSB/USB/FM) of the bands.

## Applications and Resources

The most common application for sending and receiving SSTV is [MMSSTV](https://hamsoft.ca/pages/mmsstv.php).

Other applications include [MMSSTV-YONIQ](https://hamsoft.ca/pages/mmsstv-yoniq.php) and [Ham Radio Deluxe](https://www.hamradiodeluxe.com/).
