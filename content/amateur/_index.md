# Amateur Radio

Also known as `ham radio`, Amateur Radio is a hobby where licensed hobbyists utilize various radio equipment within allocated frequency ranges. Amateur Radio is specifically non-commercial, and is intended to promote science, engineering, and building relations between countries.
